# Stage 1: Cowork GRASP Brief

## Scenario

BrightPath Workforce Solutions is a fictional business-to-business training provider. It offers customized programs in AI, data analytics, cloud computing, product management, and leadership. Its business-development team needs a consistent way to gather and organize prospect information before an intake call.

## Goal

Create a structured prospect-research packet that identifies the information needed to determine whether a prospective organization is a reasonable fit for BrightPath's customized training services.

## Resources

- Open-web research about the invented prospect, Northstar Manufacturing
- The fictional service and scoring assumptions in this brief
- Publicly available industry trends for manufacturing workforce training
- No confidential, personal, paid, or login-protected information

## Autonomy limits

Cowork may choose relevant public sources, summarize trends, identify missing information, and organize the output. It may not contact any company or person, invent facts and present them as verified, access private accounts, make a final qualification decision, or use confidential data. Any uncertain finding must be labeled as an assumption or a question for the intake call.

## Sign-off point

I will personally review the completed research packet after Cowork organizes it and before any information is used as an input for the fit-and-intake tool. I will confirm that each statement is either supported, clearly labeled as fictional, or framed as a question.

## Proof

The packet succeeds if it includes the prospect profile, likely business challenge, training need, estimated audience, possible budget range, decision-access status, implementation timeline, source or assumption labels, and at least five intake questions. I will verify that no unsupported claim is presented as fact and that every scoring field has either a usable value or an explicit “unknown.”

## Ready-to-use Cowork prompt

Using the GRASP brief above, prepare a structured research packet for Northstar Manufacturing, an invented mid-sized advanced-manufacturing prospect considering AI and data-analytics training for frontline operations employees. Search the open web only for general, current manufacturing workforce and training signals; do not claim that those signals describe this fictional company. Organize the output into: prospect profile, relevant industry signals, hypothesized needs, known versus unknown information, recommended intake questions, and structured fields for training need, audience size, budget readiness, decision authority, implementation timeline, and business challenge. Label all invented details and assumptions. Do not contact anyone or make a final sales decision. Stop after producing the packet so I can complete the required sign-off review.
