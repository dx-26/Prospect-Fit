# Cowork Output: Fictional Prospect Research Packet

## Prospect profile

Northstar Manufacturing is an invented mid-sized advanced-manufacturing company used only for this class project. The fictional company has 500–1,000 employees and is exploring workforce training for an initial group of approximately 120 operations employees.

## Relevant industry signals

Manufacturers increasingly use operational data, automation, and AI-supported tools, which can create a need for practical data literacy and change-management training. These are general industry signals, not verified claims about Northstar Manufacturing.

## Hypothesized need

The prospect may want frontline supervisors and operations employees to interpret plant data, identify performance patterns, reduce downtime, and make faster decisions. This hypothesis should be confirmed during discovery.

## Structured intake fields

| Field | Working value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Training need | AI and data analytics | Fictional assumption |
| Audience size | Approximately 120 learners | Fictional assumption |
| Budget | $25,000–$49,999 | Unconfirmed estimate |
| Decision authority | Direct access to sponsor | Unconfirmed assumption |
| Timeline | Within three months | Fictional assumption |
| Business challenge | Improve frontline data skills to reduce downtime and support faster decisions | Hypothesis |

## Known versus unknown

The scenario, company profile, and working values are fictional. Unknowns include the actual business sponsor, available budget, procurement requirements, preferred delivery format, baseline employee skills, success measures, and data-security constraints.

## Recommended intake questions

1. What business outcome should the training improve?
2. Which employee groups need training, and what is their current skill level?
3. What budget range has been discussed or approved?
4. Who will approve the program and participate in the decision?
5. When should the first cohort begin?
6. How will success be measured 30, 60, and 90 days after training?
7. Are there delivery, accessibility, scheduling, or data-security requirements?

## Human review note

This packet is suitable for testing the intake tool after a representative confirms that every detail is fictional or unverified. It should not be used to contact a real organization.
